Questions

Is there a free version?

Yes. RamWarden Lite is free. It frees memory on simple built-in rules, on one computer, with a free account and no card. Plus adds the AI warden. You can also build RamWarden yourself from GitHub.

What happens after the trial?

Plus starts and renews monthly or yearly until you cancel. Cancel any time from your account or your wallet, and your warden goes back to Lite.

Will it close something I'm using?

No. Active, pinned and playing tabs are protected, and apps only close if you put them on your list.

Is it safe for games and anti-cheat?

Yes. Games, anti-cheat, security tools and system processes are never touched.

How do I sign in?

Sign in with your wallet or your email.

What leaves my computer?

Only a short description of something your warden might close, to get a decision. Never page contents. See the privacy policy.
