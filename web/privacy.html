Privacy

Draft. Last updated 1 November 2026.

The short version

RamWarden runs on your computer. When it needs a decision about something it might close, it sends a short description of that one thing, gets an answer, and the description is forgotten. The open-source build sends nothing unless you add your own AI or API keys, and then only to the services those keys belong to.

What stays on your computer

  • Your process list, memory figures, window focus and activity history.
  • Your browser tabs. The browser helper talks only to RamWarden on your own computer.
  • Your settings, watchlist and allowlist.
  • ThreatWarden's findings and quarantined files.

What a decision sends

Only when memory is short and your warden is choosing what to close: the candidate's name (an app name, or a tab's title and site), how long it has been idle, how much memory it uses, and one of a fixed set of questions, such as whether you are likely to come back to it within a day. Request contents are not stored or logged. Logs hold a short fingerprint of your device, which questions were asked, sizes and timings.

Your account

  • Your sign-in: the wallet address or email you use. Signing in with a wallet costs nothing and gives no access to your funds.
  • Your plan: tier, billing interval, trial and renewal dates.
  • Devices you have added: a name, the platform and when each was last seen. You can remove any of them from your account page.
  • Payments: the plan, amount, method and the transaction or card-payment reference, kept for accounting. Card details go to the payment provider and are never stored by RamWarden.
  • Preferences: your warden's color and whether its comments are muted.

On-chain payments are public by nature: anyone can see a payment from your wallet to RamWarden's checkout contract.

Chatting with a warden

The pet in the corner of the site answers questions and can open a support ticket. What you type, the warden's answers, and the email you give for a reply (if any) are kept for 30 days so the team can help, or for a year after the last message if the chat became a ticket. If you're signed in, the chat is linked to your account so you can see your tickets, and the warden can read your own plan, trial date and device count to answer questions about them. It can't change anything. Your browser keeps a key to the chat in local storage so the thread is there when you come back.

This website

No analytics, no trackers and no third-party scripts. Signing in keeps a session in your browser. Your warden's color and mute setting are kept in your browser's local storage.

Contact and deletion

To have your account deleted, or for any privacy question, open an issue on GitHub.
